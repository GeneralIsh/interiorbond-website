What We Are

Not an
Agency.
An Empire.

The name Interior Bond was born in 2006 — sketched in a boarding school notebook in Ghana. The idea was always the same: a company that builds companies.

Nearly two decades later, that idea is operational. Interior Bond is a creative ecosystem and holding company — building, developing, and activating brands, media, experiences, and platforms across industries and continents.

The philosophy hasn't changed: creativity is not just expression. It is execution, systems, and cultural movement.

The Architect

General
Ishmael

Founder & Creative Director — Interior Bond

Ishmael Agyapong built Interior Bond from a boarding school sketch into a multi-venture creative ecosystem spanning two continents.

His work lives at the intersection of design, fabrication, media, and systems — not as separate disciplines but as one continuous practice. He has spent over a decade executing at the highest level: brand environments at Chase Center, Marriott, and Super Bowl activations. Platforms in production. Shows in Accra.

The empire isn't a metaphor. It's the plan.
