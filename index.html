What We Are

Not an
Agency.
A Creative
Ecosystem.

The name Interior Bond was born in 2006 — sketched in a boarding school notebook in Ghana. The idea was always the same: a company that builds companies.

Nearly two decades later, that idea is operational. Interior Bond is a creative ecosystem and holding company — building, developing, and activating brands, media, experiences, and platforms across industries and continents.

The philosophy hasn't changed: creativity is not just expression. It is execution, systems, and cultural movement.

The Architect

Ishmael
Agyapong

Founder & Creative Director — Interior Bond

Known creatively as General Ishmael

Ishmael Agyapong built Interior Bond from a boarding school sketch into a multi-venture creative ecosystem spanning the Bay Area and Ghana.

His work lives at the intersection of design, fabrication, media, and systems — not as separate disciplines, but as one continuous practice. He develops brands, builds environments, creates media, and turns ideas into operating ventures with structure behind them.

Interior Bond brings those ideas together and gives them structure, direction, and a place to grow.
